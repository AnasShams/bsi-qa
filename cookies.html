Cookies

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide web optimization, analytics, and performance reporting to the site owners.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to improve your browsing experience by:

  • Remembering your preferences and configuration settings.
  • Analyzing how you interact with our platform to optimize layouts, speed, and responsiveness across desktop and mobile screens.
  • Enabling interactive elements, such as media players, responsive navigation layouts, and integrated vector animation libraries.

The Types of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for the website to function properly. They enable basic features like page navigation and secure access to specific areas of the site. The website cannot function optimally without these.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: These help us understand how visitors interact with the website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. We use this data to improve our site architecture and professional content delivery.
  • Functional Cookies: These allow our website to remember choices you make (such as your region or text adjustments) and provide enhanced, more personalized features.

Managing and Disabling Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. You can configure your browser to refuse cookies, delete existing cookies, or alert you when a cookie is being sent.

Please Note: If you choose to disable or block cookies, some parts of this website, including responsive layout configurations and advanced web components, may not function exactly as intended.
